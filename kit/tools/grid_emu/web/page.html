The app on these screens is The Grid's own code (the staged build the Store compiles on the car) on the car's GPU draw engine compiled to WebAssembly. The car, its DME and BMW's own gauges are simulated: the numbers are a model, not your car. Nothing here talks to a car.

Car audio

The car's own volume, as its audio manager reports it. The Grid covers BMW's volume pop-up, so it draws its own bar for a moment when the level or mute changes (setting Volume bar). With nothing playing the car reads 0, and starting or stopping a source shows no bar.

Sounds

Click anywhere on the page once: browsers play sound only after a click.

The app's Sounds setting: the identity's opening, one tap sound for every button on every screen, the Engine page's answers, warnings and Track runs. No throttle or shift sounds. The app ships Off; the emulator starts On. Click a sound to hear it; it lights up when the app plays it. Use headphones or speakers with real bass: most of these sit below 120 Hz, and laptop speakers drop it. Not on the car yet: how the head unit takes a sound from an app is checked on the car first.
